Privacy

What this site collects

Nothing. There are no accounts, no forms that send data, no cookies, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking pixels. Fonts, scripts and styles are served from this site itself: visiting it makes no request to third parties.

What stays in your browser

If you switch between the light and dark themes, your choice is saved in your browser's local storage under the key vitrine-site-theme, so the next page uses it. It never leaves your device; clearing your browser data removes it. Content you paste in the playground, theme builder or stress lab is kept only in the page, and disappears when you close it.

Since the publisher collects no personal data, there is nothing to access, correct or delete on its side. For data processed by GitHub, contact GitHub through its privacy statement. You may also file a complaint with your data protection authority.
